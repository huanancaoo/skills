# Concise page and component content

## Same-viewport duplicates

Keep the facts the user needs for the task. Show each fact about the same entity at most once per viewport, including its name, current status, role, and member count. Place a task's primary action in at most one visible location. Review the header, subtitle, sidebar, form, and footer together at each supported width. Compare facts by meaning and scope, even when the wording differs. Keep record-specific values and actions with their records.

When tabs switch sections and show counts, use them as the section navigation. Omit clickable overview cards that lead to those same sections. Inside a list or table card, omit any title, total badge, or primary button already supplied by the tab or page.

Use a sidebar or nested card only for distinct content the task needs. Keep a single column when the sidebar would only repeat a status or role from the header.

## Form feedback and destructive actions

Show a field hint only to explain a disabled control, an irreversible consequence, or a business constraint. Keep it to one short sentence. Preserve field labels, accessible control names, and validation errors at their point of use.

Disable Save while the form is unchanged. Omit idle status text that merely says the data is up to date.

Give a destructive-action card the standard card border. Use destructive styling on the button and require a second step that confirms the target and consequence before executing the action.

## Content width

Left-align single-column settings forms and cap their width to suit their fields on wide screens. Give table views the full available content width.
